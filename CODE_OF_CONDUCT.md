# Code of Conduct

Sokolipa Checkout brings together developers, merchants and testers from many countries and languages. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and respected.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including in disagreement
- assuming good intent and asking before concluding
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- welcoming newcomers and helping them get started
- crediting other people's work

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- insults, harassment, trolling or personal attacks
- discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's background, identity, nationality, religion, disability, age or appearance
- sexualised language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information, including phone numbers and payment details, without their consent
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, code and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers at Snap Pay Solutions through [snapaysolutions.com](https://snapaysolutions.com) and mention Sokolipa Checkout. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
